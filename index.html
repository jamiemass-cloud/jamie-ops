About

Calm in the chaos.
Clear on the details.

"I thrive in environments that move fast, reward ownership, and need someone who can hold a lot of context without dropping threads."

I'm Jamie, an operational leader who has spent 17 years working shoulder-to-shoulder with some of the most driven founders, executives, and private principals in the world. From Web3 startups to luxury real estate to global DeFi firms, I've been the person who takes what's in your head and makes it actually happen.

I grew up in Malibu, California and spent most of my life there before making the move to Portugal, where I now live and work. That shift gave me something I didn't expect: a real feel for operating across time zones, cultures, and contexts. My clients are based all over the world and it has never once slowed us down.

My background covers both the corporate and the deeply personal. I've run company-wide planning cycles and managed private residences across multiple countries for UHNW clients. That range means I genuinely understand what it costs when operations aren't working.

I'm discreet, obsessively organized, and honestly love the kind of problems that make most people want to look away. People tend to say my energy and attitude are infectious. I show up genuinely fired up, with a good attitude no matter what's on the plate, and it has a way of lifting the people around me too. If something is complicated, messy, or moving fast, that's exactly where I do my best work.
